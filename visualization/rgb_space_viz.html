RGB Color Space Visualization

This visualizes the entire RGB color space as a 3D point cloud with semi-transparent cubes.

Each cube represents a color at position (R,G,B) and is colored accordingly.

Controls: Left-click + drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-click + drag to pan
