Organic RGB Color Space

This visualizes the entire RGB color space as a 3D point cloud with organic rendering.

Each point represents a color at position (R,G,B) and is colored accordingly.

Controls: Left-click + drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-click + drag to pan
